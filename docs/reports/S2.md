What I did:
- Found the TAE 151 `CameraLookAtTarget` look-limit writer/consumer and ported it: the event's
  four `Look*Limit` degrees now clamp the camera's orbit yaw/pitch to a cone around the
  player -> `DummyPolyID` direction, the absolute limits chasing at
  `CameraParam.lockCamParamLerpRate` (same rate the FOV/focus-height already use).
- Added `CamState.look: Option<[f32; 4]>` (pitch_min, pitch_max, yaw_min, yaw_max) plus
  `apply_look_limits` / `angle_delta` / `clamp_angle` helpers in `camera.rs`, and wired them
  into `follow_player` next to the existing TAE 151 focus code.
- `clamp_angle` handles the exe's wraparound yaw clamp and treats a full-turn range
  (`LookLeft -180` / `LookRight +180`) as no limit.
- Added `tae151_look_limits_clamp_camera_angles` to `src/sim_tests.rs`.
